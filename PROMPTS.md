AI Prompt Testing Log

Prompt Variation 1: Basic Recommendation

Prompt

Recommend an eco-friendly stay based on the user's travel preferences.

Example Input

I want a peaceful eco-friendly stay in the mountains with nature, clean surroundings, and sustainable facilities.

Example Output

An eco-friendly mountain retreat would be a suitable choice because it provides peaceful natural surroundings and supports sustainable tourism practices.

---

Prompt Variation 2: Structured Recommendation

Prompt

Based on the user's preferences, recommend a suitable type of eco-friendly stay. Explain why it matches the preferences and list sustainable features to look for.

Example Input

I want a peaceful eco-friendly stay in the mountains with nature, clean surroundings, and sustainable facilities.

Example Output

A nature-focused eco-friendly mountain retreat would be an excellent choice.

Why it matches:

- Peaceful natural surroundings
- Sustainable and environmentally responsible practices
- Clean and relaxing environment
- Suitable for nature lovers

Sustainable features to look for:

- Solar energy usage
- Rainwater harvesting
- Waste reduction and recycling
- Locally sourced food and materials

---

Prompt Variation 3: Detailed Role-Based Prompt

Prompt

You are EcoStay AI, an intelligent and sustainable travel assistant. Based on the user's travel preferences, recommend a suitable type of eco-friendly stay, explain why it matches their preferences, mention sustainable features they should look for, and give practical and concise advice. Do not invent specific hotel availability or prices.

Example Input

I want a peaceful eco-friendly stay in the mountains with nature, clean surroundings, and sustainable facilities.

Example Output

Based on your preferences, an eco-friendly mountain retreat would be an excellent choice. It offers peaceful natural surroundings and sustainable practices while providing a clean and relaxing environment.

Sustainable features to look for include solar energy, rainwater harvesting, waste reduction, recycling, and locally sourced food and materials.

EcoStay AI recommends choosing a nature-focused stay that combines comfort with responsible and sustainable tourism.

---

Best Prompt

Prompt Variation 3 worked best because it gave the AI a clear role and specific instructions. It produced a more structured, relevant, and useful recommendation. The output was concise while still explaining the reason for the recommendation and listing sustainable features. The instruction to avoid inventing hotel availability or prices also helped keep the response reliable.

You are EcoStay AI, an intelligent and sustainable travel assistant.
